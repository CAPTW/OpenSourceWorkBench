# Scope Guardrails

Allowed v0.1 work:

- PySide6 GUI shell and workflow previews.
- Plugin/add-in contracts.
- Project schema, units, materials, result datasets, and figure datasets.
- Standard/exported CAD, CAE, CFD, chemistry, `.m`, and `.mat` workflows.
- Educational demos for CalculiX, OpenFOAM templates, Cantera, and CoolProp.

Out of scope:

- Native commercial CAD import.
- Simulink or `.mlapp` support.
- Full OpenFOAM solver coverage.
- Full MATLAB proprietary toolbox compatibility.
- Industrial certification claims.
- GUI direct subprocess solver execution.
